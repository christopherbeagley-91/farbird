## Terms of service (terms of sale)

**Who you're buying from.** Farbird is a New Zealand business run by `[Farbird Limited, NZBN, registered address]`. Contact us at `[hello@farbird.co]`.

**What we sell.** Gift boxes delivered within New Zealand. Each one has the recipient's name printed on the sleeve and your card message inside. You can buy from anywhere in the world; we only deliver to New Zealand addresses.

**Prices and payment.**
- Prices are in New Zealand dollars and include delivery within New Zealand. `[Prices include GST.]`
- If you pay with a card from another country, your bank converts the price and may charge a fee.
- Payment is taken when you order. Card details are handled by Shopify Payments; we never see your full card number.

**Personalisation.**
- You're responsible for the spelling of the name and your card message. We print exactly what you type.
- We'll send you a preview of the printed sleeve before we pack. You can change the name or message until we start packing your box.
- We won't print anything offensive, unlawful, or that uses someone else's trade mark. If we can't print what you've asked for, we'll contact you, and you can change it or get a full refund.

**What's in the box.** Box contents are as described on the product page at the time you order.
- If an item becomes unavailable, we'll swap it for one of the same or higher value and similar style, and tell you in your order update.
- Food items are pre-packaged and labelled by the manufacturer. Check the packets for allergens. We can't guarantee any box is free of nuts, gluten, dairy or other allergens.
- No alcohol is included.

**Delivery.** See the shipping policy below. The last order date for Christmas 2026 delivery is **Saturday 12 December 2026**.

**Cancelling.**
- Before we print the sleeve, you can cancel for a full refund.
- After printing, the box is made for one person, so we can't cancel it. You keep your rights if something is wrong with it (see the refund policy).

**Your consumer rights.** Nothing in these terms limits your rights under the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993 or the Fair Trading Act 1986. Those rights apply because we supply in trade in New Zealand.

**Promotions and referrals.** Discount codes and referral rewards have the terms shown with them. One referral reward per customer, no cash value, and referrals must be real people using their own email. We can withdraw a reward that was gained by fake sign-ups.

**Reminders.** Reminders for birthdays, Mother's Day, Father's Day and Christmas are a free extra. You can turn them off any time. We do our best to send them on time but don't guarantee it, so please don't rely on them alone for something that can't be missed.

**Liability.** Except for your rights under New Zealand consumer law, we're not liable for indirect losses, or for delays caused by events outside our control (for example courier disruption, weather or natural disasters). If we can't deliver because of something on our side, we'll refund you.

**Privacy.** Our privacy statement explains how we handle your details and the details of the people you buy for: `[link to privacy statement]`.

**Law.** New Zealand law applies to these terms.

## Refund policy

**Something wrong with your box?** If an item arrives damaged, faulty, missing, or not as described, email `[hello@farbird.co]` within 14 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We'll replace the item or the box, or refund you, depending on what's best and what the law requires. The Consumer Guarantees Act gives you these rights, and you keep them after the 14 days. The 14 days just helps us sort it quickly.

**Late delivery.** If your box arrives after the delivery window we confirmed, and the delay was our fault, contact us. We'll refund the delivery portion or the full box, depending on how late it was and whether you still want it.

**Change of mind.** Because each box is personalised and contains food, we can't accept returns for change of mind once the sleeve is printed. Before printing, you can cancel for a full refund.

**Wrong address.** If a box can't be delivered because the address you gave was wrong or incomplete, we'll try to reroute it once. A second delivery may cost extra. If the box comes back to us, we'll refund the box minus delivery costs, unless it was our mistake.

**How refunds are paid.** To your original payment method, usually within 5–10 working days after we approve it.

## Shipping policy

**Where we deliver.** Anywhere in New Zealand, including rural addresses. We don't deliver internationally.

**How.** Boxes are packed in Auckland and sent by tracked courier `[name]`. You'll get the tracking link by email. Rural addresses can take an extra day or two.

**When.**
- Pre-sale orders placed 9 to 30 November 2026 are dispatched from `[date]` and arrive before Christmas.
- Orders placed 1 to 12 December are dispatched within `[2]` working days.
- The **last order date for Christmas delivery is Saturday 12 December 2026.**
- Delivery times are estimates. Courier networks get busy in December. If we think a box won't make it before Christmas, we'll tell you before we take payment, or give you the choice of a refund.

**Cost.** Delivery is included in the box price.

**Nobody home.** If no one is home, the courier will leave the box in a safe place or take it to a nearby depot, following their own rules. If you know the recipient won't be home, add a note at checkout.

**Heat.** In summer we pack chocolate with insulation. A box left in direct sun after delivery may still be affected, so check the delivery note.
