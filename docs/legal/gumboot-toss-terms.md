## The short version (shown next to the game)

Free to play, one throw per email. Get the gumboot in the ring and your first Christmas box is on us (10 to be won). Miss, and you still get NZ$5 off. Each throw has a 1 in 6 chance of landing in the ring. No purchase needed. [Full terms]

## Terms

1. **Promoter.** Farbird Limited [company number], [registered address], Auckland, New Zealand ("Farbird", "we").
2. **Dates.** The promotion opens when the waitlist launches on Saturday 31 October 2026 and closes at 11:59pm NZDT on Sunday 22 November 2026, or earlier once all 10 boxes have been won.
3. **Who can play.** Anyone aged 18 or over, living anywhere, except Farbird's team, the people packing for us and their immediate families.
4. **Free entry.** You don't have to buy anything to play or to win, and buying something doesn't improve your chances. To play, enter your email on the waitlist or pre-sale page and throw the gumboot. One throw per person and per email address. Throws made with multiple or fake emails, bots or scripts are void.
5. **How a winner is decided.** Whether the gumboot lands in the ring is decided at random by our system at the moment you throw, at odds of 1 in 6 per throw. How you swipe doesn't change the result. The animation shows the result; it doesn't decide it.
6. **Prizes.**
   - **Ring (up to 10 winners in total):** one Farbird Christmas box (normally NZ$89, delivery anywhere in New Zealand included). The winner chooses the box and who it's for, and orders it with their prize code by Saturday 12 December 2026.
   - **Any other throw:** a code for NZ$5 off one Farbird order placed by Saturday 12 December 2026.
   - Once the 10th box has been won, every remaining throw wins the NZ$5 code until the promotion closes. The game page shows how many boxes are left.
   - The total value of the box prizes is NZ$890.
7. **Claiming.** We email your code to the address you played with, straight away. Box prizes must be ordered by 11:59pm NZDT on Saturday 12 December 2026, or they lapse and aren't redrawn. Codes can't be swapped for cash, can't be transferred, and can't be combined with another Farbird offer (such as the Sixer or NZ$10 off).
8. **Delivery.** Prize boxes are delivered in New Zealand only, on the same terms as paid orders (see our shipping policy). If a gift in a box runs out, we swap in one of the same or higher value.
9. **Your details.** We use your email to run the promotion, send your code and send the Farbird updates you signed up for, as set out in our privacy statement (farbird.co/privacy). You can unsubscribe at any time.
10. **Fair play.** We can void a throw or cancel a prize if we reasonably believe someone broke these terms. If a technical fault or something outside our control affects the promotion, we may suspend or end it. Anything already won is still honoured.
11. **Law.** These terms are governed by New Zealand law. Nothing in them limits your rights under the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993 or the Fair Trading Act 1986.
