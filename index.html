Questions

Where do you deliver?

Anywhere in New Zealand. You can live anywhere; your card is charged in your own currency.

What does it cost?

Boxes start at NZ$49 delivered. Christmas boxes are around NZ$89. The waitlist is free, and early access gets you first pick before boxes sell out.

When can I order?

Pre-sale opens to the waitlist on 9 November, then to everyone 72 hours later. The last Christmas order date is 12 December.

Can I change the box or the message?

Yes, until we pack it. You'll get a preview of the box and the printed name before we send.

What do you do with my details?

We use them to remind you about dates and to deliver your boxes. We don't sell or share them, and you can ask us to delete them at any time.
