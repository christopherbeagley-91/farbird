Questions

The fine print, not so fine.

Where do you deliver?
Anywhere in New Zealand. You can live anywhere; your card is charged in your own currency.
What does it cost?
Boxes start at NZ$59 delivered. Christmas boxes are around NZ$89. The waitlist is free, and early access gets you first pick before boxes sell out.
When can I order?
Pre-sale opens to the waitlist on 16 November, then to everyone 72 hours later. The last Christmas order date is Saturday 12 December.
Can I add a photo?
Yes. Upload one when you order and we'll print it on a card that sits on top of the tissue. We delete it once the box is delivered.
What if I forget to order?
We'll remind you. That's sort of the whole point.
Can I change the box or the message?
Yes, until we pack it. You'll see a preview of the box and the printed name before we send.
How does the referral offer work?
Share your link. When 3 people join the waitlist through it before pre-sale closes, you get free name printing on your Christmas 2026 order. They need to be real people using their own email, and it's one reward per person. The reward has no cash value; we'll confirm it by email before you order.
What do you do with my details?
We use them to remind you about dates and to deliver your boxes. We don't sell them, and you can ask us to see, change or delete them at any time. The full detail is in our privacy statement.
