Privacy statement

Last updated 22 October 2026

The short version. We collect your details and the details of the people you're buying for so we can remind you about their dates and deliver their boxes. We don't sell anyone's details. You can see, change or delete them any time by emailing hello@farbird.co.

Who we are

Farbird is a New Zealand business based in Auckland. We send personalised gift boxes to people in New Zealand on behalf of family and friends living overseas. We're registering as a company; once that's done, our legal name and New Zealand Business Number will appear here.

We follow the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020. If you live in the UK or the European Union, we also give you the rights described under "If you live in the UK or EU" below.

Your browser also keeps your referral code and email on your device, so the page remembers you if you come back. You can clear this in your browser settings. We don't use advertising cookies on this page.

The people you buy for

When you add someone, you're giving us their details. Please only add people you're planning to send something to. We use their details only to remind you and to deliver their box, never to market to them. Every box includes a short note saying who sent it and how they can ask us to delete their details.

Emails

By joining the waitlist you agree that we can email you about pre-sale, your orders, and reminders for the dates you add. Every email comes from Farbird, includes our contact details and has an unsubscribe link that works straight away. We keep a record of when you joined and the wording you agreed to.

Who sees your details

We don't sell or rent anyone's details. A small number of services help us run Farbird and handle details only on our behalf:

  • Supabase stores the waitlist and orders on servers in Sydney, Australia.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver its fonts and code. Like any website, they see your IP address when the page loads.
  • From pre-sale: Shopify runs our shop and checkout, Shopify Payments processes payments, our email provider sends emails, and our courier and shipping software get the recipient's name and delivery address.

Some of these services are outside New Zealand. We choose providers that protect information to a standard comparable to New Zealand's. We'll only share details with anyone else if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • If you join the waitlist and never order, we delete your details and the people you added 12 months after you join.
  • If you order, we keep order records for 7 years, because New Zealand tax law requires it. We keep reminder dates until you turn reminders off or ask us to delete them.

Keeping it safe

Only the Farbird team can see waitlist and order details, and access needs a sign-in. The public website can add you to the list but can't read it. If something goes wrong and a privacy breach is likely to cause serious harm, we'll tell you and the Privacy Commissioner as soon as we can.

See, change or delete your details

You can ask to see the details we hold about you or about someone you've added, correct them, or have them deleted. Email hello@farbird.co. We'll reply within 20 working days, usually much sooner. The people you buy for can ask us the same.

If you live in the UK or EU

We use your details to provide what you signed up for and, for emails, with your agreement. You can also ask us to stop using your details, to restrict how we use them, or to send you a copy in a common format. You can withdraw your agreement to emails at any time with the unsubscribe link.

Complaints

Talk to us first at hello@farbird.co. If we can't sort it out, you can complain to the New Zealand Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz. If you're in the UK, you can also contact the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, or in the EU your local data protection authority.

Changes

If we change how we use your details, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If the change matters, we'll email you first.
